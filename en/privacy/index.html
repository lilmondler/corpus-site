Privacy policy

What moncorpus.fr does — and does not do — with your data. Short, no small print.

version
12 November 2026

In one sentence: this site does not follow you. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, nothing loaded from another server. Everything is hosted in France.

Data controller

Snezhana Galimova, publisher of the site — contact@moncorpus.fr. See the legal notice.

What this site does not do

  • No cookie is placed on your device, by us or by anyone else. That is why there is no consent banner: there is nothing to accept.
  • No analytics (no Google Analytics or equivalent), no pixels, no embedded social media buttons.
  • No font, script or image is loaded from another server: everything comes from moncorpus.fr.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared for commercial purposes.

What is processed anyway

Host’s technical logs. Like any web server, OVHcloud’s (data centre in France) records the IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type for each request. These logs are used only for security and proper operation; the host keeps them for the period required by law and then deletes them. Legal basis: legitimate interest (site security), Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your e-mails. If you write to contact@moncorpus.fr, your message and address are kept in that mailbox, hosted by OVH in France, only to answer you and follow up — for at most three years after the last message, unless a collaboration follows. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request.

Where the data is

In France, at OVH SAS (2 rue Kellermann, 59100 Roubaix). No data from this site is transferred outside the European Union.

The corpus platform

The online platform (the application where a study is carried out) is not yet open to the public: access is by invitation, as part of a pilot. Before any opening, a dedicated policy will be published, including:

  • hosting of research data in France, with a European provider, with no transfer outside the EU;
  • a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR) with each institution: for a study’s data the controller is the institution or researcher, and corpus acts as processor;
  • no built-in artificial intelligence: research data is never sent to AI services and is not used to train models, by corpus or by its subprocessors;
  • encryption of participants’ names and contact details, and an audit trail visible only to the study team.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, portability, object to it, or leave instructions about your data after your death, by writing to contact@moncorpus.fr. We answer within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

This policy may change with the project. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
