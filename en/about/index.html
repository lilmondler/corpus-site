1.who am I?

My name is Snezhana Galimova — or Jane, as you like. I’m a sociologist; I live and study in Paris. I’m interested in the sociology of everyday life: what people do, keep and throw away, and what that says about us.

2.why corpus

Like many, I’ve known the scattered study: recordings on a phone, transcripts in emails, surveys “cleaned(1)”, and the reviewer’s question that always comes in the end: where does this figure come from?

Clinical trials learned long ago to answer that question with simple rules: everything is dated and signed, the source is never overwritten. corpus tries to bring that care to sociology — without jargon, and without making it one more evening chore.

“A study is not only its conclusions.”
