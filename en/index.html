§ 01 — the problem

final version n., not found

A study is not only its conclusions. It is everything they are made of — and usually that gets lost along the way.

  1. Files get lost

    The audio on a recorder, the transcript in an email, the consent form at the bottom of a bag.

    One interview, one place: audio, text, coded participant and consent together.

  2. Versions multiply

    “final”, “final_2”, “corr”. Who changed what?

    Every processing step makes a version with its recipe. The source never moves.

  3. Provenance gets blurry

    The reviewer asks where this 38% comes from. You no longer know.

    Every figure points to the raw answer, every quote to its line.

§ 02 — what’s inside

on the table phr.

Real screenshots of the platform, version 0.6. All your fieldwork in one study rather than three programs — and everything exports to CSV, JSON, DOCX, R and GraphML.

  • From topic to conclusions, every step has a state and reasoned changes.

  • Highlight, code, keep the quote — without losing the original line.

  • A three-column table becomes a graph, with its measures.

  • Questions, collection, reasoned cleaning and export to R.

  • Where each code appears, and in whose words.

  • Inconsistencies become queries that get resolved and traced.

  • Who, what, when, before/after — in a hash chain.
